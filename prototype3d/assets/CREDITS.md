# Character asset

Worker from Ultimate Modular Men by Quaternius.
Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html
License: CC0 1.0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Downloaded from the author's linked public Google Drive folder on 2026-11-03.
Original individual glTF: Worker.gltf.

Converted to GLB, retained Idle, Run, Punch_Right, Sword_Slash, HitRecieve and Death animations, then gzip-compressed. Uses the original colored materials; no texture images are supplied in this individual character. The enemy uses a material tint. No Mixamo or Fighting Force assets are included.

Worker replaces the casual character in Heavy Swing 04. Character proportions are slightly broadened in the renderer. The pipe is enlarged and the slash clip is retimed to 0.82 seconds.
