# Character assets

Punk (player) and Swat (enemy) from Ultimate Modular Men by Quaternius.
Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html
License: CC0 1.0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Downloaded from the author-linked public Google Drive folder, 2026-11-03.

Converted individual glTF models to compressed GLB retaining Idle, Idle_Sword, Run, Punch_Right, HitRecieve, Death and Sword_Slash. Original colored materials; slightly broadened model proportions. The guard's pistol is hidden. The pipe is an original cylinder mesh attached along the right wrist's negative X grip axis. Slash is retimed to 0.82 seconds and hit timing to 0.34 seconds. The preview images are software-rendered asset inspections, not browser gameplay screenshots.

Earlier Casual_2 and Worker assets remain in repository history/files but are no longer loaded by the prototype.
